Macro Demographic Driver

Workforce Expansion: Saudi female labor participation surged from ~17% (2017) to >35% in 2023 under Vision 2030 (GASTAT Q4 2023).

Time Compression: Working women have eliminated 2-hour pre-wash rituals on weekdays, pivoting toward 30-second post-shower dropper treatments that absorb instantly before applying their sheyla.
