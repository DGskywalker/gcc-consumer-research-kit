# GCC Consumer Research Kit: Qualitative Discussion Guides (FGD & IDI)

**Study Title:** Uncovering Modern Scalp Therapies, Hair Care Rituals, and Premiumization Dynamics in the GCC  
**Geographic Markets:** Riyadh & Jeddah (KSA), Dubai (UAE), Kuwait City (Kuwait)  
**Target Participants:** Adult consumers (18–45) who actively care for their hair and scalp, regular users of oils/serums, stratified across Nationals and Long-term Expats.

---

## 1. Qualitative Research Objectives & Fieldwork Protocols

### 1.1 Objectives
1. **Unpack the "Hard Water Anxiety":** Understand the lived experience of washing hair with desalinated municipal water in the GCC, including the ritual use of shower filters, bottled water rinses, and perceived follicle damage.
2. **Deconstruct the Ritual Spectrum:** Explore the tension between *Traditional Heavy Oiling* (rooted in family heritage, amla, sidr, overnight baths) and *Modern Fast-Paced Scalp Care* (lightweight, derm-backed serums, rosemary, quick wash-out).
3. **The Hijab & Sheyla Microclimate:** Safely and sensitively explore scalp sweat, sebum trapping, friction, and root tenderness experienced under head coverings in 45°C+ summer heat.
4. **Evaluate Brand Personification & Prestige Drivers:** Contrast the functional nostalgia of legacy FMCG oils with the aesthetic aspiration of modern indie/derm brands.
5. **Concept & Texture Stress-Testing:** Gauge visceral reactions to bio-clinical claims vs royal botanical heritage claims, texture expectations, fragrance profiles, and packaging preferences.

### 1.2 Moderator Protocol & Cultural Etiquette
- **Gender Matching:** In KSA and Kuwait, female focus groups and female IDIs must be moderated by experienced female bilingual researchers (fluent in Saudi/Khaleeji Arabic and English).
- **Hospitality & Icebreakers:** Begin with traditional Khaleeji hospitality (Arabic coffee/gahwa, dates, water) to establish an informal, warm "majlis" atmosphere.
- **Tone & Psychological Safety:** Reassure participants that hair shedding, scalp flaking, and oiliness are universal natural challenges in the region, eliminating embarrassment.
- **Language Code-Switching:** Allow fluid bilingual code-switching (Arabizi, English cosmetic terms like "leave-in", "peptides", "build-up" mixed with Khaleeji dialect).

## 2. Focus Group Discussion (FGD) Guide (90 Minutes)

**Group Composition:** 6–8 participants per group.  
**Session Length:** 90 minutes.

### Section 1: Welcome, Introduction & Warm-Up (10 Mins)
- **Moderator Introduction:**
  > *"Salam Alaykum everyone, and welcome! Thank you so much for joining us this evening. My name is [Moderator Name]. Tonight, we are here for an informal, relaxed conversation about our daily routines, hair and scalp care, and the everyday realities of living in [Riyadh / Dubai / Kuwait]. There are no right or wrong answers—we are not here to judge any brand or routine. We just want to hear your honest personal experiences."*
- **Participant Icebreaker:**
  - Introduce yourself: First name, how long you've lived in this city, and if you had to describe your hair's personality in one word today, what would it be?

### Section 2: Everyday GCC Environment & The "Water Audit" (20 Mins)
- **The Climate & Water Reality:**
  - Let's talk about living here: When you step out in July or August, or when you take a shower in your home, what happens to your hair and scalp?
  - *Probe:* How would you describe the shower water here compared to when you travel abroad to Europe, East Asia, or back to your home country?
  - *Probe on Desalinated Water:* Do you notice a difference in how shampoo lathers? Do you feel mineral buildup or calcium crust on your scalp?
  - *Show of Hands / Tally:* How many of you use a shower filter (e.g., Blu, Philips, Pure Blue)?
    - *For filter users:* Does it actually solve the problem, or is it a psychological band-aid?
    - *For non-users:* What prevents you? (Hassle, skepticism, plumbing restrictions).
  - *The "Bottled Water Rinse" Phenomenon:* Have you ever rinsed your hair with drinking bottled water (e.g., Evian, Mai Dubai, Nova, Al Ain)? When and why?
- **The Hijab / Sheyla / Headwear Microclimate (Female Groups):**
  - For those of us who wear a hijab or sheyla throughout the working day: What happens underneath after 8 hours in an air-conditioned office followed by outdoor parking?
  - *Probe:* Root tenderness? Sweaty scalp? Greasiness at the crown while the tips are bone dry?
  - What do you do the moment you come home and take off your hijab? What is your "scalp relief" moment?

### Section 3: Vanity Cabinet Tour & The Weekly Hair Calendar (25 Mins)
*Exercise: Participatory Mapping (Whiteboard / Flipchart)*
- **The Weekly Hair Calendar:**
  - Let's draw a typical week from Saturday to Friday. When do you wash your hair? How often?
  - Walk me through your oiling or scalp treatment routine:
    - *Moment 1: Pre-wash deep bath.* What oils do you use? How long do you keep it on? (30 minutes? Overnight? Under a warm towel or plastic cap?)
    - *Moment 2: The Wash-Out battle.* How many shampoo lathers does it take to get traditional oil out?
      - *Probe:* What is the emotional feeling when oil won't wash out? (Frustration, feeling dirty, limp hair).
    - *Moment 3: Post-wash / Leave-in.* Do you put anything directly on your clean damp scalp? Why or why not?
- **Traditional Oils vs Modern Serums (The Generational Shift):**
  - What did your mother or grandmother teach you about hair oils? What ingredients did you grow up with? (Amla, sweet almond, black seed, castor, olive oil).
  - How has your own routine changed compared to your mother's?
  - Do you still use traditional green/amber oils? If not, why did you stop? (Smell, staining bedsheets, difficult wash-out, heaviness).

### Section 4: Brand Archetypes & Projective Personification (15 Mins)
*Projective Technique: The Riyadh / Dubai Café Gathering*
- Imagine all the hair care brands were invited to an upscale coffee shop (e.g., EL&N or % Arabica) in Riyadh or Dubai.
  - **Dabur Amla walks in:** Who is she? How is she dressed? What is she drinking? How do people look at her?
    - *Expected Probes:* Nostalgic auntie, hardworking, strong, but pungent smell, old-school.
  - **Kérastase or Olaplex walks in:** Who is she? How is she perceived?
    - *Expected Probes:* Salon-obsessed, high-income, modern professional, clinical, sophisticated.
  - **Gisou or Fable & Mane walks in:** Who is she?
    - *Expected Probes:* Aesthetic influencer, Instagram/TikTok viral, smells gorgeous, honey/rosemary, Sephora haul.
  - **Mielle Organics walks in:** What is her reputation?
    - *Expected Probes:* The rosemary scalp sensation, TikTok sensation for thinning edges, intense herbal kick.

### Section 5: Concept Evaluation & Price Corridor Probe (15 Mins)
*Moderator distributes Concept Stimulus Cards: Concept A (PureDesert Bio-Clinical) and Concept B (Al-Reem Royal Heritage).*
- Take 2 minutes to read Concept A silently. Jot down your first immediate impression.
  - *Immediate Gut Check:* What word jumps out at you?
  - *Probe on "Chelating Hard-Water Defense":* What does this term mean to you? Does it feel credible, or is it marketing jargon?
  - *Probe on "Copper Peptides & Rosemary":* How do you feel about clinical ingredients in hair oils?
- Now let's look at Concept B (Royal Heritage Sidr & Black Seed).
  - How does this feel compared to Concept A?
  - *Probe on Sidr (النبق / السدر):* What is your cultural association with Sidr? (Sunnah, holy botanical, natural cleansing, grandmother's remedy).
  - *The Scent Factor:* How important is it that your hair smells like Taif Rose or Oud under your sheyla?
- **Price Sensitivity Probe (150 SAR / AED for 100ml):**
  - If you saw this bottle priced at 150 SAR/AED on a shelf at Sephora or Boots:
    - What would you expect it to do for that price?
    - At what price would you think: *"That's suspiciously cheap for a real treatment"*?
    - At what price would you put it back on the shelf and say: *"No way, that's absurd"*?

### Section 6: Wrap-Up & The "Magic Wand" Exercise (5 Mins)
- If you had a magic wand and could create the ultimate scalp product that solved your #1 daily hair frustration in the Gulf, what single miracle would it perform?
- Thank you, warm closing, distribution of research incentives.

## 3. In-Depth Interview (IDI) Guide (60 Minutes)

**Target Participant:** One-on-one deep dive with consumers experiencing acute hair thinning, postpartum hair loss, chronic scalp sensitivity, or high-income prestige beauty buyers.  
**Session Length:** 60 minutes.

### Section 1: Personal Hair Journey & Intimate Scalp History (15 Mins)
- **Life Stage & Hair Changes:**
  - Let's go back 3–5 years: How has your hair changed? Have you experienced major life events that impacted your hair? (Moving to the GCC, pregnancy/postpartum, weight loss/Ozempic, intense career stress, thyroid issues).
  - When did you first notice that your scalp needed care separate from your hair strands?
  - Have you ever visited a dermatologist, trichologist, or aesthetic clinic for your scalp? What treatments have you explored? (PRP injections, mesotherapy, Minoxidil, prescription steroid drops).

### Section 2: The Emotional Burden of Hair Thinning (15 Mins)
- **The Psychology of the Hairbrush & Shower Drain:**
  - Describe the feeling when you look at the shower drain or your brush after washing:
    - *Laddering Probe (Means-End Chain):*
      - *Level 1 (Attribute):* Hair strands collected in the drain.
      - *Level 2 (Consequence):* Loss of hair volume, widening parting line, scalp visibility under bright lighting.
      - *Level 3 (Emotional Impact):* Feeling premature aging, loss of feminine identity, anxiety in public social gatherings.
      - *Level 4 (Core Value):* Self-worth, control, youthful confidence.
  - How does this affect how you style your hair when attending family weddings, dinners, or work meetings?

### Section 3: The Vanity Exploration & Product Disappointment Audit (15 Mins)
- **Show and Tell (In-home or Virtual Vanity Tour):**
  - Show me the products currently sitting on your bathroom counter or in your shower caddy.
  - Which product has been your biggest disappointment? Why?
    - Did it break you out? Cause forehead acne? Weigh your hair down? Smell unpleasant?
  - Have you ever experienced scalp clogging or folliculitis from heavy traditional oils? How did you resolve it?

### Section 4: Deep Evaluation of Concepts & Trade-Offs (10 Mins)
- Detailed exposure to Concept A and Concept B.
- *Trade-Off Probe:* If you had to choose between a product that smells like French luxury perfume but only stops 30% of shedding, versus an unscented clinical serum that stops 80% of shedding but looks like medicine, which would you pick?
- *Packaging & Delivery Mechanism:* Dropper vs spray vs pump—what is your daily ergonomic preference when applying directly to scalp partings?

### Section 5: Conclusion & Reflection (5 Mins)
- Summary of key insights, validation of core dilemmas, warm thank you.
